<h1 align="center">MacBulwark</h1>

<p align="center">
MacBulwark is a firewall for Mac: you decide, app by app and domain by domain, what may reach the internet, and your rules keep working even when the app is closed. This repository is where MacBulwark gets its updates: each release here is offered automatically to every copy of MacBulwark. To get MacBulwark, go to <a href="https://monokern.app/macbulwark">monokern.app/macbulwark</a>.
</p>
